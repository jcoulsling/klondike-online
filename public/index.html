Pokémon Klondike Version online

An unofficial fan project. You need your own copy of Pokémon FireRed (US, version 1.0) and the patch file. The game is made and played in this page, on your device. No game, patch or save is uploaded. Only where you stand on the map is sent, so that the people in your room can be seen walking in your game.

Not connectedKeys: arrows, Z = A, X = B, Enter = Start, Backspace = Select

    You can see the other players walk around on the same map. You can chat and share a whiteboard with the room below the game. You cannot trade and battle yet.

    This page does not contain, host or share any game. The emulator is mGBA (MPL 2.0, mgba.io). A note on the use of an LLM (AI assistant): this page and its server were written with the help of Claude, an AI assistant from Anthropic, and checked by running them.
